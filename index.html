03Why it's still unsolved

A decade of fit tech missed by 5×.

Fit and sizing tools promised to cut returns 30–40%. They delivered 2–8%. The gap isn't effort — it's the kind of data they learn from.

What they learned from
Engagement data
  • Clicks, scans, size-picker taps, body scans
  • Captured before the outcome is known
  • Doesn't transfer between brands, blocks, or fits
What Sartori learns from
Outcome data
  • What was kept, what came back, and the stated reason
  • Captured after the truth is in
  • Ground truth that generalizes — and compounds per brand
Engagement data doesn't transfer. Outcome data does.

The founder's peer-reviewed paper, “Beyond the Click”, formalizes this divergence as the wear gap — the commercial thesis has an academic footing.

11The Moat

Every brand makes it harder to catch.

The defensibility isn't the models — it's the outcome graph underneath them. It can only be accumulated one proven fix at a time, and a new entrant starts at zero.

01 · Data that can't be bought

A triplet only exists after a brand acts on a finding. It can't be scraped, licensed, or inferred from catalog data.

02 · Cross-brand priors

A fix proven at one brand becomes the starting prior for the next. Competitors begin every engagement at zero.

03 · Speed to data

No integration means a new brand contributes outcome data in three weeks, not two quarters. The graph grows at sales velocity.

04 · Embedded in decisions

Once the roadmap drives size curves and supplier QA, replacing Sartori means losing the causal baseline those decisions are measured against.
